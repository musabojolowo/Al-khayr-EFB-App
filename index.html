About Al-Khayr EFB Tournament

Al-Khayr EFB Tournament is a community eFootball competition platform supporting multiple tournament formats — single leagues, home & away leagues, and group-stage championships — all run from a single, unified scoreboard.

Only one tournament is ever active at a time, but every tournament keeps its own announcements, rules, fixtures, results and standings safely stored, so organisers can run tournament after tournament without losing any history.

Registration, fixtures, results and standings are all managed and kept accurate by the Al-Khayr EFB admin team — check the Announcements page for the latest updates.
